Golden Hour — a GTA 6 cover studio. Design your own Vice City poster: 6 layouts, 5 sunset grades, your photos or ours, export in 3 post sizes. Vanilla JS, offline, no libraries.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts --port 8080
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 — pick a layout, a grade and photos, type your title, and export the cover as PNG (1080×1080, 1080×1350, or 1920×1080).
