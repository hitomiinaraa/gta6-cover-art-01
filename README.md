Golden Hour — a GTA 6 cover studio. Turn your photos into a Vice City sunset cover: six multi-panel layouts, five neon grades, bold 3D lettering, press quote, seal, barcode and billing — export as PNG in 1080×1080, 1080×1350 or 1920×1080. Works fully offline in a desktop browser.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 — pick a layout and grade, drop in your photos, edit the caption, and export the PNG.
