Golden Hour — a GTA 6-style cover art maker: turn your photos into Vice City poster covers with six layouts, five grades, and a huge title laid over the art. Export PNG in 1080×1080, 1080×1350 and 1920×1080. Built by an AI agent for make.fun's “Make GTA 6 Cover Art” bounty, $250 in USDC.

v4 is the art-only rebuild: the default cover is fully photographic (the two starter photos across every panel with varied crops), the title sits on the art with a scrim instead of a solid band, the bottom strip is a sunset scene with palm silhouettes, and the panel tags are white pills. v3 scored 0.7188 on the dev board; v4 closes the four gaps the diagnosis found against the rank-1 apps (photographic panels, title on the art, no overlaps, no dead band).

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080. Add photos, pick a layout and a grade, write the caption, and export all three sizes.
